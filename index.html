What is Genshin Impact and who created it ?

Genshin Impact is a free-to-play fantasy open-world action role-playing game developed by miHoYo, a Chinese company now known internationally as HoYoverse. The company is also known for other successful titles such as Honkai Impact 3rd and Honkai: Star Rail, which share similar art styles and storytelling depth.

Released on September 28, 2020, the game has remained popular for over five years, thanks to its continuous updates, new regions, and engaging events. It combines exploration, combat, and story-driven quests in a large open world called Teyvat.

The game's financial model is based on the gacha system, which allows players to obtain characters and weapons through randomized pulls using in-game currency. Despite being free to play, the game has a reputation for its high-quality graphics, music, and character design.

What do players do in Genshin Impact ?

In Genshin Impact, players explore the vast world of Teyvat, filled with mysteries, puzzles, dungeons, and unique quests. The main gameplay focuses on exploration and real-time combat, where players can control a party of up to four characters, each with different abilities and elements.

Every character is tied to one of the seven elements: Anemo (Wind), Geo (Earth), Electro (Lightning), Dendro (Nature), Hydro (Water), Pyro (Fire), and Cryo (Ice). Combining these elements creates special Elemental Reactions — a key feature in the game's combat system.

Players can:

  • Explore new regions and hidden areas,
  • Fight enemies and bosses using elemental combos,
  • Gather resources for crafting and cooking,
  • Complete story quests and character events,
  • Enter Domains to earn rewards and character materials.

Beyond combat, the game also includes relaxing activities such as cooking, fishing, and building your own realm inside the Serenitea Pot — a personal housing system.

The Story of Teyvat

The story takes place in Teyvat, a world divided into seven nations, each ruled by a god known as an Archon.

These nations are:

  • Mondstadt,
  • Liyue,
  • Inazuma,
  • Sumeru,
  • Fontaine,
  • Natlan,
  • and Snezhnaya.

Each Archon represents one of the seven elements and influences the culture, environment, and traditions of their people.

Above Teyvat lies Celestia, a mysterious floating realm said to be the home of the gods and of mortals who have ascended to godhood. Long ago, an independent nation called Khaenri'ah was destroyed, leading to the current state of the world.

The main story follows the Traveler, one of two twins who arrive in Teyvat after journeying between worlds. Separated by a mysterious god during the fall of Khaenri'ah, the Traveler awakens centuries later and begins a journey to find their lost sibling — joined by Paimon, a small floating companion who acts as a guide and friend.

What I Enjoy in This Game ?

What makes Genshin Impact really fun for me is just how free you are to do your own thing. I love wandering around the open world, checking out the stunning landscapes, and picking which characters I want to play with. The music, story, and overall vibe make it feel like a real adventure, and I'm always finding cool little things around Teyvat. On top of that, the events and updates keep things fresh, so there's always something new to try. The combat is super satisfying, and mixing elements in fights is just plain fun. Plus, I never get bored of the puzzles and hidden treasures, they make exploring feel really rewarding.
