Mondstadt - The Nation of Freedom

Mondstadt, the city of open skies and wind-swept plains, is home to Barbatos, the Anemo Archon and God of Freedom. Its people value liberty above all, enjoying music, festivals, and self-expression without heavy governmental control.

Inspired by Germany and medieval Western Europe, Mondstadt is famous for its vineyards, breweries, and freedom-loving citizens.

The nation itself is often called the Nation of Freedom, reflecting its ideals.

Life here is peaceful, with gentle winds carrying the scent of the city's wine and the sound of music through the streets, creating a sense of liberation for travelers and locals alike.

Notable characters include Venti (the playful bard and Archon in disguise), Jean (the acting Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius), and Diluc (wealthy winery owner and vigilante).

  • Venti

    Venti is the Anemo Archon and God of Freedom, who presides over the city of Mondstadt. Known for his freedom-loving nature, he appears as a wandering bard, but secretly holds the divine power to control wind. He is one of the original members of "The Seven," powerful deities who rule the world of Teyvat.

  • Jean

    Jean Gunnhildr is an Anemo character and the Acting Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius. A swordswoman with a dedication to protecting the city of Mondstadt, she is also the older sister of Barbara.

  • Diluc

    Diluc Ragnvindr is a Pyro character, known as the master of the Dawn Winery and the owner of the Angel's Share Tavern. He is a former member of the Knights of Favonius who left after a past incident, but continues to secretly protect Mondstadt as the "Darknight Hero".

Liyue - The Nation of Contracts

Liyue is a bustling harbor city inspired by China, blending traditional architecture with vast trading ports. Its people honor Rex Lapis, the Geo Archon and God of Contracts, who ensures agreements and promises are kept.

Associated with the Geo element, Rex Lapis embodies stability, law, and prosperity. The nation itself is known as the Nation of Contracts, reflecting its mercantile nature and focus on trade, culture, and honor.

Liyue's streets are alive with merchants, lantern festivals, and mountain vistas that remind residents of the Archon's enduring protectio

Notable characters include Zhongli (the Geo Archon), Ningguang (a shrewd businesswoman controlling Liyue's commerce), and Xiao (a vigilant Yaksha).

  • Zhongli

    Zhongli is the mortal persona of the ancient Geo Archon Morax. On the surface, he is a consultant for the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor in the nation of Liyue, but in reality, he is a god who created the nation and ruled it as Rex Lapis. He is known as the God of Contracts and has stepped down from public rule to experience life as a mortal.

  • Ningguang

    Ningguang is a Geo character, holding the position of Tianquan of the Liyue Qixing and owner of the Jade Chamber. She is a powerful and wealthy businesswoman known for her intelligence, elegance, and strategic mind. She is an important political figure in the game's story who governs the city of Liyue alongside the other members of the Liyue Qixing.

  • Xiao

    Xiao is an Anemo character, known as the Vigilant Yaksha. He is an adeptus, a god-like being who has defended the land of Liyue for over 2,000 years, serving the Geo Archon Morax. Due to his long battles against demonic forces, he is plagued by the "karma" of defeated gods, which causes him pain.

Inazuma - The Nation of Eternity

Inazuma, a stormy archipelago inspired by Japan, worships Raiden Shogun, the Electro Archon and God of Eternity. Her rule emphasizes unchanging order, aiming to preserve an eternal vision for her nation.

Raiden Shogun wields the Electro element, and her strict policies impact every part of society, from politics to culture. The nation is known as the Nation of Eternity, a place of lightning, cherry blossoms, and disciplined warriors.

Inazuma's islands are surrounded by thunder and sea spray, offering breathtaking views but also a sense of isolation and order that mirrors the Shogun's philosophy.

Notable characters include Raiden Ei (the Electro Archon), Yae Miko (head shrine maiden of Grand Narukami Shrine), and Kamisato Ayato (leader of the Yashiro Commission).

  • Raiden Ei

    Raiden Ei is the true name of the Electro Archon and ruler of Inazuma, also known as Beelzebul. She is often confused with the Raiden Shogun, a puppet she created to rule in her place, though Ei is the playable character often referred to as "Raiden Shogun" after the story's events.

  • Yae Miko

    Yae Miko is an Electro character who serves as the head priestess of the Grand Narukami Shrine and the owner of the Yae Publishing House. She is a cunning, mischievous, and intelligent kitsune (fox-like yokai) who is a close friend and servant to the Electro Archon, Raiden Ei.

  • Kamisato Ayato

    Kamisato Ayato is a Hydro character, serving as the current head of the Kamisato clan and the Yashiro Commissioner in Inazuma. He is the older brother of Kamisato Ayaka, known for being cunning and strategic while keeping a low public profile.

Sumeru - The Nation of Wisdom

Sumeru is inspired by Middle Eastern and South Asian cultures, a center for scholars and knowledge. The nation worships Lesser Lord Kusanali, the Dendro Archon and God of Wisdom, associated with the Dendro element.

As the Nation of Wisdom, Sumeru values learning, research, and philosophy above all else. Its residents attend the renowned Sumeru Akademiya, where scholars from across Teyvat come to study.

The cityscape is lush, with verdant jungles and vibrant markets filled with books, plants, and ancient artifacts, reflecting the nation's deep connection to nature and knowledge.

Notable characters include Nahida (the Dendro Archon), Alhaitham (a Akademiya student), and Scaramouche/Wanderer (a mysterious figure).

  • Nahida

    Nahida is the Dendro Archon and God of Wisdom of the Sumeru region, a character who uses a Catalyst weapon. Known as Lesser Lord Kusanali, she is the youngest of the seven Archons, created from Irminsul to succeed the previous Dendro Archon.

  • Alhaitham

    Alhaitham is a Dendro character, known for his role as the Scribe of the Sumeru Akademiya. He is a brilliant but pragmatic scholar who uses a sword and focuses on powerful on-field damage through his elemental abilities. His character is inspired by the Arab mathematician and physicist Ibn al-Haytham.

  • Wanderer

    The Wanderer is an Anemo character who was formerly a major antagonist named Scaramouche, the Sixth Fatui Harbinger. After a storyline in Sumeru where he was defeated and had his memories erased, he chose to adopt the name Wanderer and became a wandering figure.

Fontaine - The Nation of Justice

Fontaine is inspired by France, known for its advanced technology, law systems, and cultural sophistication. The nation worships Focalors, the Hydro Archon and God of Justice, who embodies fairness, law, and accountability.

Hydro powers govern much of Fontaine's aesthetic, giving it a maritime and elegant character. As the Nation of Justice, its courts, canals, and civic architecture reflect balance and societal order.

Fontaine's streets are lined with fountains, mechanical innovations, and elegant bridges, showing off the harmonious blend of art, science, and justice.

Notable characters include Furina (the former Hydro Archon), Neuvillette (a top judicial officer) and Chlorinde (the strongest Champion Duelist in Fontaine).

  • Furina

    Furina is a Hydro character who is the human form of the former Hydro Archon, Focalors. She is known for her flamboyant public persona as the Hydro Archon, which was a performance to save her people from a prophecy, and is a powerful off-field support character in gameplay.

  • Neuvillette

    Neuvillette is a Hydro character, known as the Chief Justice (Iudex) of Fontaine and the Hydro Dragon Sovereign. He is a serious and impartial figure who leads the Court of Fontaine, but beneath his human guise is a powerful ancient dragon who was recently restored to his full authority.

  • Chlorinde

    Clorinde is an Electro character, known as the strongest Champion Duelist in Fontaine and a former bodyguard for Furina. She is a major character in the Fontaine chapter of the game, with a history alongside Navia, and is associated with the Marechaussee Hunters.

Natlan - The Nation of War

Natlan, inspired by volcanic and Indigenous American cultures, is a fiery and passionate nation. Its ruler, Mavuika, the Pyro Archon and God of War, embodies courage, strength, and martial skill.

Pyro power fuels the nation's culture of combat, celebrations, and volcanic energy. The nation itself is called the Nation of War, reflecting its warrior traditions and fiery temperament.

Natlan is full of vibrant colors, volcanic landscapes, and energetic festivals, all reflecting the nation's intense, untamed spirit.

Notable characters include Mavuika (the commanding Archon), Kinich (a Dendro warrior), and Iansan (a tribal leader and Electro warrior).

  • Mavuika

    Mavuika is the current Pyro Archon and the God of War for the nation of Natlan. Originally a human named Mavuika, she was chosen to become the Archon 500 years ago after winning a warrior's tournament. Known by the Gnostic name Haborym, she is revered by her people for her leadership in defending Natlan against the Abyss.

  • Kinich

    Kinich is a Dendro Claymore character, known for his utilitarian philosophy, combat skills as a Saurian Hunter, and his companion K'uhul Ajaw. He is a character from the Natlan region, from the tribe known as the Scions of the Canopy.

  • Iansan

    Iansan is an Electro Polearm character, originating from the nation of Natlan. She is a fitness instructor and champion from the Collective of Plenty.

Snezhnaya - The Nation of Winter

Snezhnaya, inspired by Russia, is a frozen land of political intrigue and military power. Its ruler, the Tsaritsa, the Cryo Archon, governs through the Fatui, her devoted and ruthless agents.

Snezhnaya is associated with the Cryo element, giving the land a cold, icy aesthetic. Known as the Nation of the Tsaritsa, it emphasizes loyalty, strategy, and careful control.

The autonomous region Nod-Krai exists in the south, maintaining semi-independence but culturally tied to Snezhnaya.

Snow-covered landscapes, harsh winters, and intricate palaces reflect the Tsaritsa's icy rule and the calculated power dynamics of Snezhnaya.

Notable characters include Tsaritsa (the Cryo Archon), Tartaglia (Childre, the 11th Fatui harbinger), and Arlecchino (The Knave, the 4th Fatui harbinger).

  • Tsaritsa

    The Tsaritsa is the Cryo Archon of Snezhnaya, the main antagonist of Genshin Impact, and the leader of the Fatui. She is on a mission to "burn away the old world" by gathering the Gnosis of the other Archons in rebellion against the divine, though her motivations are shrouded in mystery.

  • Tartaglia

    Tartaglia, also known as "Childe" and by his birth name Ajax, is the Eleventh of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers and a Hydro character. He is known for his love of battle, his ruthless and unpredictable nature on the battlefield, and his loyalty to his family, especially his younger siblings.

  • Arlecchino

    Arlecchino is the Fourth Fatui Harbinger, also known as "The Knave". She is a Pyro character who is the head of the House of the Hearth, an orphanage in Snezhnaya where she is called "Father" by the children she oversees.
